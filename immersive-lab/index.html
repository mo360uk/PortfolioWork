Mo360 Immersive Media Lab

Spatial Research Tools & Creative Media Courses

Mo360 Immersive Media Lab is the research and teaching arm of Mo360 Immersive Studio. We build practical spatial tools for real world site work and teach focused, studio led courses in fulldome cinema, projection mapping, generative art, interactive installations, and spatial sound.

Research & Development

Studio Built Spatial Tools

Alongside artistic commissions, the lab develops practical software tailored to heritage documentation, property surveys, and aerial capture.

Research Project 01

Mo360 Spatial Survey Studio

A unified workspace for capturing, organising, and reviewing building conditions by linking 2D architectural floor plans directly with 360 degree room photography.

Designed for architects, heritage teams, and site managers who want a clear visual record of a space without paying for restrictive third party subscriptions.

  • Place exact room markers directly onto building floor plans
  • Walk through full 360 degree photography from any pinned location
  • Record site observations, snag notes, and shareable client summaries
Active studio prototype available for private demonstrations. Request Demo
Research Project 02

Mo360 Autonomous Drone Mission Studio

A visual flight planner for mapping roofs, coastlines, and historic structures with consistent photo overlap before the aircraft ever leaves the ground.

Draw the survey area on a satellite map to preview flight lines, check estimated flight time and battery needs, and keep missions aligned with UK altitude limits.

  • Define custom survey boundaries over high resolution satellite imagery
  • Estimate coverage detail, photo intervals, and flight duration automatically
  • Export clean flight plans ready for field aerial photography and 3D mapping
Interactive browser prototype ready to explore. Open Planner
Teaching & Workshops

Structured Creative Media Courses

Five practical six week programmes led by Mohamed Elwakil, available for independent learners, visiting university workshops, and bespoke group training. Open any course curriculum below to inspect the weekly structure.

Course 01 6 Weeks · 6 Modules

Fulldome Cinema & Real Time Immersive Worlds

Learn how to compose, light, and direct visual stories made specifically for 180 degree dome screens. Designed for artists, filmmakers, and creative technologists moving from flat frames into surrounding architectural cinema.

What You Will Achieve
  • Understand spherical framing, horizon comfort, and audience sightlines inside a dome
  • Build responsive 3D scenes in TouchDesigner and Unreal Engine suited to wide curved projection
  • Deliver a finished high resolution dome master film ready for festival or venue screening
View 6 Week Curriculum
  • Week 1 Foundations of Real Time 3D Composition Navigating real time scene workspaces, framing for overhead spaces, and building your first architectural form.
  • Week 2 Spherical Lighting & Atmosphere Shaping mood, depth, and colour across a full hemisphere without jarring seams or eye strain.
  • Week 3 180 Degree Fulldome Projection Framing Converting 3D worlds into clean circular dome masters while keeping motion smooth and comfortable.
  • Week 4 Real Time Environments in Unreal Engine Designing richly lit virtual worlds, particles, and natural camera movement for dome playback.
  • Week 5 Live Visual Control & Performance Connecting live video feeds and external controllers so visuals respond in real time during screenings.
  • Week 6 Mastering & Screening Delivery Final colour checks, high resolution rendering, and preparing exhibition files for dome venues.
Course 02 6 Weeks · 6 Modules

Architectural Projection Mapping & 3D Heritage Scanning

Turn real buildings, sculptures, and historic interiors into living canvases. This course bridges 3D scanning and site specific projection so you can map light accurately onto physical surfaces.

What You Will Achieve
  • Capture clean 3D scans of facades, arches, and heritage objects using accessible camera and LiDAR tools
  • Align projected light precisely onto physical architecture for indoor galleries or outdoor facades
  • Plan projector placement, brightness, and site rehearsals with confidence
View 6 Week Curriculum
  • Week 1 Capturing Real Spaces in 3D Planning photographic and LiDAR scans in natural light to produce tidy, usable architectural models.
  • Week 2 Point Clouds & Visual Textures Transforming raw heritage scans into poetic point clouds and stylised surface layers.
  • Week 3 2D Surface Masking & Alignment Isolating windows, columns, and relief details so animations sit naturally inside architectural lines.
  • Week 4 3D Projector Calibration Matching your virtual camera to a real projector lens for depth accurate mapping across complex geometry.
  • Week 5 Multi Surface Storytelling Choreographing pacing, shadows, and transitions across rooms or multi projector installations.
  • Week 6 On Site Technical Setup & Showcase Preparing reliable show playlists, backup plans, and live testing workflows for public events.
Course 03 6 Weeks · 6 Modules

Generative Sacred Geometry & Real Time Visual Art

Explore the timeless mathematical harmony of Islamic and natural geometric traditions and bring those patterns to life as fluid, real time 3D artwork.

What You Will Achieve
  • Construct symmetrical 8, 10, and 12 fold geometric star patterns and architectural lattices
  • Animate thousands of repeating forms smoothly in real time using visual programming tools
  • Package finished generative pieces for galleries, live stages, or standalone display screens
View 6 Week Curriculum
  • Week 1 Geometric Proportions & Radial Symmetry Translating classical compass and grid proportions into clean, editable 3D star rosettes.
  • Week 2 Sculpting Organic Surfaces & Shaders Working with procedural light, brass and stone finishes, and continuous mathematical form.
  • Week 3 Visual Programming Foundations Building modular real time visual systems in TouchDesigner and vvvv without writing heavy code.
  • Week 4 Fluid Particle & Pattern Motion Creating calm, meditative movement and breathing rhythms across complex geometric fields.
  • Week 5 Building Clean Artist Controls Designing simple sliders and presets so you can improvise live during performances or exhibitions.
  • Week 6 Standalone Exhibition Export Optimising frame rates and exporting self contained artworks ready for gallery playback.
Course 04 6 Weeks · 6 Modules

Interactive Sensors, Movement & Responsive Installations

Create artworks that notice and respond gracefully to human presence. Starting with nothing more than a standard laptop webcam, you will progress to depth sensing and physical room interaction.

What You Will Achieve
  • Track hand gestures and body movement smoothly using accessible camera based tools
  • Turn raw sensor readings into calm, natural visual responses rather than jittery motion
  • Design reliable interactive installations built to run unattended in public spaces
View 6 Week Curriculum
  • Week 1 Zero Hardware Hand & Body Tracking Using a standard webcam to detect expressive hand gestures and posture in real time.
  • Week 2 Smoothing Motion & Expressive Feel Refining movement data so on screen light and particles feel fluid, weighty, and intentional.
  • Week 3 Depth Sensors & Physical Triggers Exploring room scale presence zones with depth cameras and simple physical microcontrollers.
  • Week 4 Connecting Movement to 3D Worlds Linking live audience gestures to dynamic particle systems, lighting cues, and sound.
  • Week 5 Designing Public Interaction States Creating welcoming idle states, smooth visitor transitions, and uncluttered crowd interactions.
  • Week 6 Gallery Reliability & Live Testing Stress testing your installation for varying lighting conditions and full day public exhibitions.
Course 05 6 Weeks · 6 Modules

Spatial Audio & Sensory Friendly Audiovisual Performance

Combine surrounding soundscapes with gentle, reactive visuals designed with neurodivergent comfort and wellbeing at heart. Ideal for sound artists, musicians, and visual designers creating restorative spaces.

What You Will Achieve
  • Drive subtle visual motion directly from live acoustic instruments, voice, and ambient recordings
  • Position sound in three dimensional space for both headphones and multi speaker rooms
  • Design paced, sensory aware audiovisual performances free from harsh strobe or audio fatigue
View 6 Week Curriculum
  • Week 1 Listening to Frequency & Musical Phrasing Separating warm bass, melody, and gentle texture so visuals breathe naturally with live music.
  • Week 2 Pacing & Sensory Comfort Design Using slow breathing rhythms, warm colour palettes, and gentle contrast for neurodivergent accessibility.
  • Week 3 3D Soundscapes for Headphones & Rooms Placing acoustic elements smoothly around the listener using binaural and surround techniques.
  • Week 4 Synchronising Live Music & Visuals Linking live audio sessions and performance controllers so sound and image stay tightly aligned.
  • Week 5 Interactive & Generative Sound Environments Building evolving ambient sound worlds that respond softly to movement or audience energy.
  • Week 6 Staging a Calm Live Performance Rehearsing transitions, sound checks, and audience welcome practices for live sensory sessions.

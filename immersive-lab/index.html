Research & Development

Studio Built Spatial Tools

Alongside artistic commissions, the lab develops practical software tailored to heritage documentation, property surveys, and aerial capture.

Research Project 01

Mo360 Virtual Tour App & Spatial Survey Studio

An in house Android field capture and survey application linking 2D architectural floor plans directly with live 5.4K 360 degree Ricoh Theta room photography.

Built for Mo360 Studio heritage documentation, property walkthroughs, and site condition records without relying on third party cloud subscriptions.

  • Live 360 degree camera viewfinder with instant on location spot snapping
  • Direct room pin linking across 2D architectural floor plans and 360 spheres
  • Automatic high resolution equirectangular export straight to device gallery
Proprietary in house Android APK. Private demonstrations available on request. Request Private Demo
Research Project 02

Mo360 Autonomous Drone Mission Studio

An in house Android flight planning and DJI Mobile SDK cockpit application for mapping roofs, coastlines, and historic structures with consistent photo overlap.

Calculates boustrophedon and 3D crosshatch flight grids, ground sample distance, and battery budgets while enforcing UK CAA 120 metre altitude limits.

  • Direct DJI RC-N1 USB controller bridge and VirtualStick waypoint execution
  • UK postcode, address, and exact GPS coordinate survey grid targeting
  • Export ready mission files in Litchi CSV, Rainbow KML, and DJI JSON formats
Proprietary in house Android APK. Private demonstrations available on request. Request Private Demo
Fulldome & Artistic Research

Collective Immersion & Spatial Practice

All studio research, fulldome cinema experiments, spatial audio studies, and spatial software tools live together here under Mo360 Immersive Media Lab.

Research Focus 01

Fulldome & Shared Cinema

Investigating 180 degree dome framing, curved projection optics, and communal storytelling where audiences experience immersive worlds together rather than isolated inside individual headsets.

Research Focus 02

Spatial Sound & Sensory Comfort

Designing surrounding acoustic fields and slow visual pacing rooted in neurodivergent accessibility, calm nervous system regulation, and inclusive cultural spaces.

Research Focus 03

Heritage Archiving & Generative Art

Uniting 3D digital conservation, sacred geometric traditions, and real time graphics to keep fragile cultural heritage open, living, and accessible to future generations.

Statement of Practice
"True immersion is not the isolation of people inside sealed headsets; it is the shared transformation of physical space into a living, responsive architectural canvas."

Working across Plymouth, Devon, and London, Mo360 Immersive Media Lab bridges real time graphics, spatial sound, and digital heritage conservation for cultural commissions, university teaching, and research residencies.

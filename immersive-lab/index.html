MODULE 01 Navigating TouchDesigner & Your First 3D Scene
Week 1 · 90 mins ▾

Learning Outcome: Understand TOP, CHOP, SOP, MAT, and COMP operator families and build a rotating 3D geometric vault.

  • Sphere SOP (Radius 7.5, Rows 32, Columns 64)
  • Geometry COMP (geo1)
  • Camera COMP (cam1 at 0, 0, 0 looking +Y/+Z)
  • Light COMP (light1)
BEGINNER STEP BY STEP WIRING & PARAMETERS
  1. STEP 01Open TouchDesigner, select all default nodes with Ctrl A, and press Delete for a clean canvas.
  2. STEP 02Press Tab, switch to the SOP tab, place a Sphere SOP, and set Radius to 7.5 to match the 15 metre Market Hall dome.
  3. STEP 03Right click the right output connector of the Sphere SOP and attach a Geometry COMP named geo1.
  4. STEP 04Press Tab, open COMP, and place a Camera COMP and Light COMP beside geo1.

MODULE 02 CubeMap Rendering Inside the Render TOP
Week 2 · 90 mins ▾

  • Parameter: Render Mode ·> Cube Map
BEGINNER STEP BY STEP WIRING & PARAMETERS
  1. STEP 01Press Tab, open the TOP family, and place a Render TOP.
  2. STEP 02In the Render TOP Common page, set Resolution to 2048 by 2048.
  3. STEP 03On the Render page, change Render Mode from Normal to Cube Map so TouchDesigner renders all six faces around your zenith camera.
  4. STEP 04Confirm Camera points to cam1 and Geometry points to geo1.

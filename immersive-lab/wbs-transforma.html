THE WBS TRANSFORMA

Created during Mohamed Elwakil's 2014 PhD research at Cardiff (University of Wales) and exhibited at National Museum Wales (Amgueddfa Cymru). Inspired by the Ancient Egyptian Ushabti servant figure, the stacked sculptural sequence is presented here as an interactive 3D studio piece and 360-degree lighting film, prepared at 260 mm exhibition scale and 180 mm ceramic scale.

EXHIBITION PROVENANCE, FABRICATION EDITIONS & STUDIO FILM

2014 ARCHIVE · NATIONAL MUSEUM WALES

Cardiff PhD Research & Ushabti Form

Created in 2014 as part of doctoral research in Cardiff, Wales and exhibited at National Museum Wales (Amgueddfa Cymru). Inspired by an Ancient Egyptian Ushabti figurine from the museum collection, the work fans and stacks multiple silhouettes into a continuous ascending sculpture.

GALLERY FINISH & FABRICATION EDITIONS

Sandstone & Ceramic Print Editions

Prepared as unified solid studio print masters in upright museum orientation, preserving the carved relief and continuous spiralling base for powder sandstone and ceramic fabrication.

  • 260 mm Sandstone / Powder Edition Full gallery scale (`260 x 251.8 x 222 mm`) prepared as a unified continuous surface for high-detail powder printing.
  • 180 mm Ceramic / Clay Studio Edition Scaled edition calibrated for clay extrusion and ceramic firing stability, resting level on its coiled base.
  • Interactive Digital Edition Lightweight upright GLB with real time ceramic glaze, sandstone finish, and interactive studio lighting.

Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE DATE: 20 NOVEMBER 2026 · UK GDPR & DATA PROTECTION ACT 2018

1. Data Controller

Mo360 Immersive Tours is operated by Mo360 Immersive Studio, based in Plymouth, Devon, United Kingdom. For all data protection enquiries, please contact info@mo360immersivestudio.co.uk or call +44 7568 668918.

2. Personal Data We Collect

When you book a guided heritage or cultural walking tour directly or through authorised booking partners (including GetYourGuide), we collect only the details necessary to deliver your tour safely:

  • Full name and booking reference number.
  • Contact email address and mobile telephone number for meeting point updates or weather alerts.
  • Accessibility, mobility, or dietary notes you choose to share for your comfort during the tour.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To confirm, manage, and deliver your booked guided tour and companion 360 digital experience.
  • To send essential pre tour operational notifications (such as meeting point instructions or schedule adjustments).
  • To comply with UK tax, insurance, and public safety record keeping obligations.

We never sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

4. Retention & Your UK GDPR Rights

Booking records are retained only as long as required for legal and accounting purposes (typically up to 6 years under UK HMRC rules). Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing info@mo360immersivestudio.co.uk.

ABOUT YOUR GUIDE · MOHAMED ELWAKIL (MO)

VISUAL ARTIST, CREATIVE TECHNOLOGIST, CURATOR & CREATIVE DIRECTOR

Mohamed Elwakil is a Devon based visual artist, creative technologist, and curator, and founder of Mo360 Immersive Studio in Plymouth.

Before launching Mo360 Immersive Tours, Mohamed worked and volunteered at The Box Museum in Plymouth, led gallery tours at KARST and MIRROR, guided international audiences at Ars Electronica, and led historical tours across the Egyptian Museum in Cairo as well as walking tours across Plymouth and Exeter.

Every experience pairs local architectural and maritime storytelling with custom Mo360 digital archives and complimentary 360° photography.

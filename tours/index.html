VISUAL ARTIST, CREATIVE TECHNOLOGIST, CURATOR & CREATIVE DIRECTOR

I am a Visual Artist, Creative Technologist, Curator, and Creative Director based in Devon, and founder of Mo360 Studio, creating interactive and immersive arts experiences across the South West.

Long before launching Mo360 Immersive Tours, I spent years giving guided tours inside major museums, galleries, and international festivals. My experience includes working and volunteering at The Box Museum in Plymouth, leading gallery tours at KARST and MIRROR, guiding visitors at Ars Electronica, and giving artistic and historical tours across the Egyptian Museum in Cairo alongside walking tours through Plymouth and Exeter.

Living in Devon deepened my appreciation for South West coastal architecture, moorland prehistory, and maritime heritage. Integrating custom spatial apps, archival comparisons, and complimentary 360° photography makes every walk vivid and lasting.

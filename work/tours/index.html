About Your Guide · Mohamed Hossam Elwakil (Mo)

Visual Artist, Creative Technologist, Curator & Creative Director

I am a Visual Artist, Creative Technologist, Curator, and Creative Director based here in Devon, and the founder of Mo360 Studio, where we create interactive and immersive arts experiences.

Long before launching Mo360 Tours, I spent years giving guided tours inside major museums, contemporary galleries, and international festivals. My background includes working and volunteering at The Box Museum right here in Plymouth, leading gallery tours at KARST and MIRROR in Plymouth, guiding visitors at international exhibitions including Ars Electronica, and giving artistic and historical tours across the Egyptian Museum in Cairo, alongside leading walking tours through Plymouth and Exeter.

Whenever I have travelled across the world, I have always been drawn to heritage sites, historic architecture, coastal landscapes, and outdoor nature walks. Living in Devon for a long time, I fell deeply in love with the history, nature, and coastline of Plymouth and the South West. I started these tours to share that genuine passion with both local residents and visitors. Through my studio's work in creative technology and interactive arts, I also integrate custom interactive apps and spatial storytelling into my tours to make every walk and museum visit even more vivid and memorable.

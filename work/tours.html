Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining us on tour.

Where do we meet in Plymouth?

Our primary departure location is right outside Plymouth Railway Station main entrance (convenient for train and bus arrivals). We also offer pickups outside Sherwell United Church on North Hill (convenient for university students) or direct pickup from central Plymouth hotels and Derriford.

What if it rains on the day of the tour?

Cornish weather can be unpredictable, but our tours are rainproof! The Eden Project features colossal heated indoor rainforest and Mediterranean biomes that are completely dry and warm. At Heligan, we provide umbrellas and have warm indoor tearooms. The Bodmin steam train features fully enclosed, heated vintage carriages.

Can I book on GetYourGuide or directly with you?

Both! If you hold GetYourGuide gift credits or prefer international payment options, you can book via our verified GetYourGuide link. If you prefer to book directly with Mohamed, submit the form below or message via WhatsApp for zero booking fees.

How does the free 360 VR Spatial Album work?

Because Mohamed runs Mo360 Immersive Studio, he captures professional high resolution 360 degree spherical photos of your group at the most breathtaking vantage points. Within 24 hours of your tour, you receive a private, interactive web link you can open on any smartphone, tablet, or VR headset.

What is your cancellation policy?

We follow the UK gold standard: 100% full refund if you cancel up to 24 hours before your tour departure time. No hidden cancellation penalties.

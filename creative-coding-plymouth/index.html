CREATIVE CODING PLYMOUTH

An open, welcoming monthly community meetup in Plymouth for visual artists, developers, musicians, students, and complete beginners who want to explore creative coding, generative art, interactive sound, and experimental digital tools together.

1 Community Meeting Every Month
01 · MONTHLY CADENCE

One Meetup Every Month

We gather once a month in Plymouth to share work in progress, talk through creative tools, and learn from each other in a relaxed, zero pressure setting. Exact dates and venues are posted on Meetup.com.

02 · OPEN TO EVERYONE

Artists, Coders & Beginners

Whether you write shaders, build installations in TouchDesigner or Unreal, experiment with p5.js and Processing, or have never written a single line of code, you are welcome to join, listen, or share.

03 · SHOW & TELL

Peer Exchange & Collaboration

Bring a laptop sketch, a work in progress, a question, or just curiosity. Every session leaves open time for short informal demos, feedback, and meeting fellow creative practitioners across Devon.

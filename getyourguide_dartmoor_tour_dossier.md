## 5. Full Product Description (Rich Narrative for GetYourGuide Portal)
Leave the coastal bustle of Plymouth behind and venture into the primordial, mist crowned wilderness of Dartmoor National Park, one of the last great open expanses of untamed moorland in southern England. As your luxury small vehicle climbs above the tree line, vast skies open over sweeping valleys carpeted with purple heather, golden gorse, and rugged granite outcrops carved by millions of years of weather. Dartmoor is a living, breathing landscape of ancient myth, where wild grazing ponies wander alongside bubbling crystal clear rivers and wind carved stone towers dominate the skyline.

Follow in the footsteps of celebrated authors and folklorists as we explore the evocative landscapes that inspired Sir Arthur Conan Doyle to write the greatest Sherlock Holmes adventure of all time, The Hound of the Baskervilles. Stand beneath the towering, skeletal pillars of Hound Tor, an eerie fortress of weathered granite where local legends tell of a pack of spectral black hounds guarding the ghostly moor. Nearby, wander through the preserved stone hut circles of an abandoned Bronze Age village, touching megalithic stones placed by Devon settlers over three thousand years ago.

Travel across the high undulating moor to Postbridge, home to the finest surviving clapper bridge in Britain. Walk across giant slabs of granite that have carried tin miners, packhorses, and travelers across the East Dart River since the medieval era. Keep your camera ready as Dartmoor ponies, hardy sheep, and Highland cattle frequently graze along the riverbanks. Mohamed, your local host and creative storyteller, will provide fascinating context on Dartmoor tin mining heritage, granite quarrying history, and the delicate moorland ecosystem.

Descend into the emerald valley of Widecombe in the Moor, an idyllic village celebrated in traditional Devon folk song. Admire the soaring granite tower of the Church of Saint Pancras, affectionately known as the Cathedral of the Moor, and explore charming local artisan shops. Relax with an authentic Devon cream tea featuring fresh warm scones, thick local clotted cream, and strawberry preserve at a historic moorland tavern. Return to central Plymouth feeling deeply refreshed by the wild moorland air, with an exclusive collection of high resolution 360 degree spatial photographs delivered directly to your smartphone.

## 6. Step by Step Timetable Itinerary
• 09:15 AM: Pickup and Welcome outside Plymouth Railway Station Main Entrance (PL4 6AB) or Sherwell United Church North Hill. Meet Mohamed and board your climate controlled small group vehicle.
• 09:55 AM: Ascent to High Dartmoor via Yelverton moorland gate. Transition into open access moorland with stunning initial photo viewpoints over the Walkham and Meavy valleys.
• 10:25 AM: Hound Tor Discovery Walk & Bronze Age Village. Guided walk among the majestic granite towers of Hound Tor and the preserved stone dwellings of the medieval village. First 360 degree spatial group panorama capture.
• 11:35 AM: Haytor Rocks Panoramic Summit & Historic Granite Tramway. Visit Haytor, the most famous tor on Dartmoor. Option for an easy climb to the summit rock for 360 degree coastal panoramas, and examine the unique granite rails of the 1820 Heytor Tramway.
• 12:45 PM: Widecombe in the Moor Village & Devonshire Cream Tea Break. Leisurely midday break in this enchanting valley village. Visit the Cathedral of the Moor (Church of Saint Pancras), browse independent craft shops, and enjoy a traditional Devon pub lunch or cream tea.
• 02:00 PM: Postbridge Ancient Clapper Bridge Exploration. Walk out upon the massive medieval granite clapper bridge spanning the East Dart River. Observe wild Dartmoor ponies grazing in the surrounding riverside meadows.
• 02:45 PM: Scenic Moorland Safari Drive via Two Bridges & Princetown. Journey across the untamed interior plateau, passing through Two Bridges and viewing the historic high moor town of Princetown, legendary Dartmoor Prison, and ancient peat commons.
• 03:45 PM: Return Arrival in Central Plymouth. Smooth drop off at Plymouth Railway Station (PL4 6AB) with convenient onward bus and rail connections.

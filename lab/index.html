Core Research Vectors

Our R&D lab moves beyond traditional flat screen boundaries into collective, spatial, and acoustic reality.

Research Vector // 01

Fulldome 360° Cinema & Domes

Exploration of curved projection geometry, optical calibration, and narrative pacing within hemispheric planetariums and inflatable art pavilions (such as Devon Dome).

  • Multi-projector edge blending & warp correction
  • Fisheye lens equidistant & equisolid mappings
  • Collective communal gaze dynamics vs. isolated VR
Research Vector // 02

Ambisonics & Acoustic Localization

Higher-Order Ambisonics (HOA) sound field synthesis designed to interact physically with dome geometry, psychoacoustics, and immersive multi-channel speaker rings.

  • 3rd & 4th Order Ambisonic spatial encoding
  • Binaural HRTF convolution for spatial headphone study
  • Physical dome standing-wave frequency resonance modeling
Research Vector // 03

Generative GPU Shaders & Math

Real-time GLSL fragment shaders, raymarching signed distance fields (SDF), and parametric mathematical surfaces rendered in real-time WebGL.

  • Real-time 60–145 FPS browser GPU computation
  • Audio-reactive vertex displacement topologies
  • Non-Euclidean spatial interfaces & digital twins
Statement of Practice
"Immersion is not the isolation of human beings inside sealed headsets; true immersion is the collective transformation of physical space into a living, responsive architectural canvas."

At Mo360 Immersive Studio, our research is anchored in the belief that digital technology must reintegrate with communal physical architecture. While personal computing has increasingly driven audiences into isolated rectangular rectangles or closed VR goggles, fulldome cinema and architectural spatial computing invite groups of individuals to share simultaneous sensory awe.

Through our practice in Devon and London, we bridge the mathematical precision of real-time computer graphics with the organic warmth of sound, light, and shared human presence.
